# DELTA 0.9.0, a release film from v0.8.0 to main (2026-10-27)

`examples/delta/film-0.9.0.tsx`: what changed between the v0.8.0 tag and
main, as the next release (0.9.0) in the DELTA series. 91.2 s, 1920×1080,
30 fps, cut to a 100 BPM score from `make-score-0.9.0.py`.

## Specification

- The release as a broadcast ("ON AIR"). The open lights a control room's
  multiviewer (PVW, PGM, and the twelve features as twelve sources running
  their demos), counts down on PGM, takes the title to air, and dives into
  PGM by the first chapter's downbeat. Each feature is then a program: label,
  headline, diff and rundown on the left, the demo on the program monitor,
  the chapter's sentence lit word by word under it (`CaptionTrack`), and the
  feature's name on a `LowerThird`. Programs cut on the downbeat behind an
  orange stinger. MORE is twelve cards of two beats. The outro holds the end
  card for a bar, pulls back onto the wall, and goes off air on the last
  bar; the last frame is the `poster` composition.
- Features, in three blocks: what goes on screen, LOWER THIRD (#442),
  CALLOUT (#443), CAPTIONS (#451 #499), DIALOGUE (#452 #453 #466); the
  mix, FADES (#444), DUCKING (#465 #466 #463), LOUDNESS (#479 #480); and
  the editor writing the TSX, PROPERTIES (#525 #516 #508 #550), CANVAS
  (#530 #535), EDIT SCOPE (#555), TIMELINE (#552), ADD (#553). MORE:
  EMPHASIS (#450), LIGATURES (#459), THEME (#481), VIDEO SIZE (#553),
  CHAPTERS, SCENE CLOCK, FAILED FRAMES, WEB AUDIO, EASING, ZUNDA, TOOLING,
  FIXES.
- Regrouped after #555, #552 and #553 landed on main: the three editor
  features got programs of their own, and EMPHASIS, LIGATURES and THEME
  moved to MORE as cards that run their program demos scaled and sped up.
  MORE grew by two bars (twelve cards), so the film by 4.8 s.
- The PR list (`v0.9.0/prs.ts`) is every PR merged after the v0.8.0 tag
  except the 31 Renovate dependency updates (51 PRs), from `gh pr list`.
- The film uses what it shows: the score fades with `fadeIn` / `fadeOut`
  and ducks 12 dB under three real voice lines from
  `examples/assets/voice-folder/shizuku` (`duck` / `duckGroup`, one line a
  `DialogueSeries` line with `spokenText`, `subtitlePages` and `emphasis`);
  the composition declares `chapters={SCENE_LIST}`; the rundown and MORE's
  SCENE CLOCK read `useScene(SCENE_LIST, { clock: 'root' })` from inside
  sequences; shapes take `<Theme>` color names; the export runs with
  `--loudness streaming`; VIDEO SIZE draws `examples/greenscreen`'s footage
  with `<Video width>` and `<Video height>`.

## The short (2026-10-28)

`shorts-0.9.0.tsx` plays the same cut, score and mix at 1080×1920, laid out
again the way 0.7.0's short is (`v0.9.0/layout.ts`, `useLayout()`): the
wall has 9:16 PVW and PGM monitors side by side and the sources in four rows
of three, so the dive into PGM lands on a portrait program
(`ShortProgram.tsx`: the lower third at the safe area's top left with
`placement="top-left"` and `safeArea="vertical-social"`, the headline, the
monitor at the frame's width, the caption, the diff). MORE's cards and the
end card are stacked inside the safe area; `ShortFrame.tsx` fills the edges
(the ON AIR lamp, the mix's bands, the program number, its name). The
stinger is the film's, scaled to the frame's height.

## Implementation notes

- `v0.9.0/multiview.tsx`: the wall and `wallCamera(dive)`, which shrinks the
  visible width geometrically from the wall to PGM so the dive keeps one
  speed. The open shows PGM's first chapter through `<FreezeFrame>` at the
  chapter's frame 0, so the hand-off to the real program is seamless.
- Demos draw into a 1000×563 stage and take `{ frame, live }`. On the
  multiviewer `live` is false: DIALOGUE draws the portrait without its
  `DialogueSeries`, so one voice plays. Voices other than DIALOGUE's are
  `<Audio>` at the root (`Sound` in `reel.tsx`), not in the demos.
- `v0.9.0/mix.ts` holds the bed's props and `bedGain(frame)`, built with
  `duckEnvelope()` from the voices' intervals and evaluated with the
  destination key's easing (`Easings.easeInOut` for `ease-in-out`, #474), so
  FADES and DUCKING draw the curve the mixer plays.
- Voices are leveled to -15 dBFS with `matchVoiceLevels()`; the recordings
  speak at about -24 dBFS, which left them only ~5 dB over the ducked bed.
  The score's breakdown is on LOUDNESS, not under DUCKING, so the dip is
  heard; the glockenspiel lift starts with the editor block (PROPERTIES).
- EDIT SCOPE, TIMELINE and ADD are drawn after the user guide's
  `03-preview.md` sections (the labels, the written source, the order of
  the gestures); the price tags are real `Panel`s and ADD's ellipse and
  text real `Ellipse` and `Text` at the values written.
- `v0.9.0/loudness.ts` copies the export's `loudness` report
  (`integratedLufs` -14.05, `truePeakDbtp` -1.0, `gainDb` 2.36). The picture
  does not change the mix, so it only needs re-measuring when the score,
  fades, ducking or voices change.
- Text with a highlight word in a headline must be a `<Span>` itself:
  `hi('…')` returns one, as `<Text>` rejects other elements as children.
- `fontFeatures` merge tag by tag: a `<Span>` inside `{ calt: false, liga:
  false }` text needs both turned on for JetBrains Mono's `!==` to ligate
  fully.
- The Japanese subtitle file carries each chapter's explanation and, over
  them, the three spoken lines (DIALOGUE's pages come from the
  `DialogueSeries` under a `lang="ja"` sequence, so no `ja-JP` file is
  written).
